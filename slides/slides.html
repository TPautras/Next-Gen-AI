A real wrong agreement: C10 (age at the time of the order)

modelvalue

Cause

Both queries keep "year difference 25 and birthday passed" and drop "year difference 26, birthday ahead". They agree because they share the same incomplete definition of a completed age, even though they come from two providers and two families.

What would have caught it

  • Our panels abstain, but a panel of north, qwen and dots would have published 119410 with 2 votes.
  • Boundary fixture: two 25-year-olds, one per branch, expected 1100. The reference query gives 1100, and qwen and dots give 100.
  • Synthetic DEMO-VOTE: > instead of >= on the start date gives 0, 0, 100, and the 2-of-3 vote keeps 0.

Decision

  • Error cost (assumption): about 50 EUR for a wrong figure shown to the customer, about 5 EUR for an abstention handled by an analyst.
  • 20b alone: 3/20 wrong, about 7.5 EUR per question of expected error, against 0.00013 USD of tokens. The token price is not what decides.
  • Period and product questions go to gpt-oss-20b; any age filter goes to the 2-of-3 panel; without a majority, a human decides. Observed: 0 wrong, 2/20 abstentions.
  • Cost per question: N/A. Needs a priced third family or a dated north-mini-code price.

What this does not cover

  • Twenty questions, one draw: no general error rate, no permanent ranking, no error bound guaranteed by the quorum.
  • The rule is fitted on the same twenty results (in-sample). One account per provider was used.
  • Simulator: availability and resets are assumed, and the question family is assumed known before routing.
